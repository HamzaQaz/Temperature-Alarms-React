# 01 — Monthly report email

**What to build:**
On the first of each month, one email per Campus (or one district-wide) summarising last month: the hottest and most humid closets with their peaks, incident count and total time in each Condition, Offline and Sensor fault time per Device, and closets that ran warm most of the month. Evidence to take to facilities for HVAC work.

**Blocked by:** none (uses the mailer from notifications)

**Status:** done

**GitHub:** #15

- [x] A pure builder from last month's incidents and Readings to {subject, text, html}, like `notificationEmail.ts`; times and days in the server's zone
- [x] A monthly job, queued through the outbox (a `report` row) so it retries and shows on Settings like any email; sent once per month even across restarts
- [x] `NOTIFY_MONTHLY_REPORT=true`, with deploy flag and `info`
- [x] A "Send last month's report now" button on Settings
- [x] Each closet links to its History for the month
- [x] Tests on the builder with a fixed month of data; a seam test that the job sends once

## Open questions

- Who gets it: `NOTIFY_TO`, per-Campus recipients (notifications-2 03), or a separate list for managers?
- Is "ran warm most of the month" a fixed threshold, or warm relative to the other closets?

## Comments

**2026-10-07 — owner decisions (triage).** Sent to `NOTIFY_TO` (no separate list, not per Campus). "Ran warm most of the month" is a fixed threshold: most Readings within a few degrees of that closet's own Hot warning threshold, not relative to other closets.

**2026-10-07 — built by an agent; awaiting review.** ADR 0008 (new "Update 2026-10-07: Monthly report"); CONTEXT.md, **Monthly report**.

**What was built.** `backend/src/monthlyReportEmail.ts`, beside `notificationEmail.ts`, is the pure builder: from a month (`LocalMonth`, cut in the server's zone by the new `localMonth` and `monthBefore` in `localDay.ts`), each Device's Readings summed up (count, how many were warm, its hottest and most humid Reading) and the Incidents that overlap the month, it makes `{subject, text, html}`: `[Temperature Alarms] Monthly report, September 2026: 5 incidents, 1 closet ran warm`, then a line on what it covers and five sections: incidents by Condition with their count and time clipped to the month (an ongoing one runs to the month's end), the five hottest and five most humid closets with their peak and when, the closets that ran warm most of the month, and each Device's Offline and Sensor fault time, longest first. Every closet line links to its History (`PUBLIC_URL/history/<id>?date=YYYY-MM-DD`); in HTML the closet name is the link. It reuses `degrees`, `percent`, `when`, `duration`, and `escapeHtml` from `notificationEmail.ts` (now exported). `backend/src/monthlyReport.ts` is the job: `monthlyReportData` reads the month (one range of each Device's index at a time, Bench left out), `queueScheduledReport` records the month in the new `monthly_reports` table and queues a `report` row in the outbox in one transaction under the month's primary key, and `runMonthlyReportPass` (every 15 s, started in `index.ts` whenever email is on) queues the scheduled report when `NOTIFY_MONTHLY_REPORT` is on, then claims one due `report` row (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, read committed, like the Incident sender), builds and sends it to `NOTIFY_TO`, and marks it sent, or records the failure with the outbox's backoff and gives up after a day. Migration `0017-monthly-report` adds the `report` kind, makes `notifications.incident_id`, `device_id`, and `level` nullable, adds `notifications.report_month` (YYYY-MM), and creates `monthly_reports (month, queued_at)`. The Incident sender's change is one condition, `kind <> 'report'`, on its due query. `config.ts` reads `NOTIFY_MONTHLY_REPORT` (`true` or `false`, any case; empty is off) as `notifications.monthlyReport`; Compose passes it as `${NOTIFY_MONTHLY_REPORT:-}`, both `.env.example` files list it. `POST /api/notifications/report` (Admin token, one a minute, 409 while email is off) queues last month's report and answers 202 `{month, queuedAt}`; the status gains `monthlyReport`. Settings, Notifications: an outline "Send last month's report now" button beside "Send test email", a line saying whether the report goes out on the 1st, and a status line naming the month queued; the status is read again at once and 20 s later, so it shows the report waiting, then sent. Deploy: `--notify-monthly-report on|off` in `deploy.sh` and `deploy.ps1`, in the email group (`NOTIFY_KEYS`, so `--smtp-host off` empties it and it is refused with email off), asked by the interactive email setup, and `info` says `monthly report on` or `off`. README, DEPLOYMENT.md, the deploy skill, CONTEXT.md and ADR 0008 updated.

**Decisions beyond the ticket.** Binding, from the owner (triage): sent to `NOTIFY_TO`, one district-wide email; "ran warm" is a fixed threshold near the closet's own Hot warning. Mine, open to the owner: "within a few degrees" is 3 °F, and "most of the month" is more than half the closet's Readings that month (exactly half is not), counting a Reading at or above `HOT_WARNING_F` − 3 (79 °F at the default); thresholds are global today, so every closet's own Hot warning is `HOT_WARNING_F`, but the builder takes it per closet. Off by default; the interactive setup offers yes. Ignored without `SMTP_HOST`, like `NOTIFY_REMIND_HOURS`, not refused. The deploy flag takes `on` or `off` (case-sensitive) and writes `true` or `false`. The scheduled report is queued by the first pass from midnight on the 1st in the server's zone, or the first after the server comes back, so a server down on the 1st sends it late rather than never; turned on mid-month, last month's goes out at once. The month is remembered in `monthly_reports` rather than read from the outbox, because the outbox prunes sent rows after a week. The button queues one whatever the setting, and does not count as the month's scheduled report. The email is built when it is sent, not when queued, so a retry carries the same month's data as it then stands. A report is its own email, never coalesced with Incident emails, and sent by the report job rather than the Incident sender, to keep the sender change to one line while the per-Campus recipients work changes it. Devices on the Bench are left out, as from every Notification; a Device added after the month ended with no Readings or Incidents in it is left out too. Incidents overlapping the month all count, including one carried over from the month before. The peak lists name five closets each, the earlier peak first on a tie. History shows one day, so "its History for the month" links to the day the line is about: the peak's day, a warm closet's hottest day, and the day a Device's first Offline or Sensor fault stretch in the month began. The month's Readings are read a Device at a time (a few hundred short statements for 100 Devices) so the report never holds up ingest; humidity is not in the covering index, so the most humid lookup reads rows.
