# 02 — Reminders for long incidents

**What to build:**
An incident still open and unacknowledged after `NOTIFY_REMIND_HOURS` (say 4) emails once more, then again every `NOTIFY_REMIND_HOURS`, so a closet hot all weekend is not forgotten after the first email. ADR 0008 left reminders for later.

**Blocked by:** 01 (an acknowledgement stops reminders)

**Status:** done

- [x] `NOTIFY_REMIND_HOURS` in `config.ts` (empty or 0: off), passed by Compose, set by `deploy install --notify-remind-hours`, shown by `info`
- [x] The Offline sweep (or a sibling pass) queues a `reminder` row in the outbox for each open, unacknowledged incident past its next reminder time, under the same Device row lock; `incidents.last_reminded_at` keeps it from queuing twice
- [x] Bench Devices stay silent, as in ADR 0008
- [x] Reminders coalesce with everything else; subject `[Temperature Alarms] Still open: CHS IDF 2 Hot critical, 6 h`
- [x] Tests: queued once per period, not after acknowledgement, not after close, not on restart (the period counts from the incident, not the process)

## Comments

**2026-10-07 — built by an agent; awaiting review.** ADR 0008 (new "Update 2026-10-07: Reminders"); CONTEXT.md, **Reminder**.

**What was built.** Migration `0013-incident-reminders` adds `incidents.last_reminded_at` (DATETIME, NULL until the first) and the `reminder` kind to `notifications.kind`, guarded so a run that died before being recorded repeats cleanly. `NOTIFY_REMIND_HOURS` is read in `config.ts` as `notifications.remindHours` (whole hours, 0 to 168; empty or 0 is off, the default), listed commented in both `.env.example` files, and passed by Compose as `${NOTIFY_REMIND_HOURS:-}`. `outbox.ts` gains the pure `reminderDue(start, lastRemindedAt, now, periodMs)`: due once a whole period has passed since the last reminder (or the start), answering the point on the incident's own schedule (start plus a whole number of periods) to store. `outboxStore.enqueueReminders` runs at the end of each Offline sweep pass while reminders are on: one query finds open, unacknowledged incidents past their next reminder, Bench Devices aside; each Device's are then rechecked in one transaction under its row lock (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`, the lock ingest and the sweep take), with the incident rows locked too (`FOR UPDATE OF i`), a `reminder` row is queued at the incident's worst level, and `last_reminded_at` moves on in the same transaction. The sender drops (deletes) a claimed reminder whose incident has since been acknowledged or ended (`dropStaleReminders`), before deciding whether the batch is ready. `notificationEmail.ts`: a lone reminder's subject is `[Temperature Alarms] Still open: CHS IDF 2 Hot critical, 6 h`; its entry reads "Hot critical: still open after 6 h" and adds "Not acknowledged yet: acknowledging it on the Dashboard stops these reminders"; a digest adds `(2 still open)` (or `all still open`) beside the resolved count. How long is counted from the start to when the row was queued (`queuedAt`, the row's `created_at`), in whole hours, then days (`openFor`). Deploy: `--notify-remind-hours N` in `deploy.sh` and `deploy.ps1` (0 to 168; with email off it is refused like the other `--notify-*` flags; `--smtp-host off` empties it with the group), the interactive email setup asks for it offering 4 (or the current value), and `info` (and the install summary) says `reminders every 4 h` or `reminders off`. DEPLOYMENT.md, the deploy skill, README, CONTEXT.md and ADR 0008 updated.

**Decisions beyond the ticket.** Decided by the coordinator, open to the owner: one period for every Condition and level; `NOTIFY_REMIND_HOURS` empty or 0 is off and is the default, and `deploy install` offers 4 when email is set up interactively; no cap on reminders per incident (acknowledging stops them); reminders are queued only while notifications are on (without `SMTP_HOST` the setting is ignored, like `NOTIFY_COALESCE_SECONDS`, rather than refused). Mine: at most 168 hours (a week), refused above at startup and by the deploy scripts. The schedule is anchored at the incident's start, so after a gap (the server down, reminders just turned on, or a pass late) one reminder covers every missed period and the next falls back on the schedule, rather than one per missed period or a schedule drifting by the sweep's lateness; a shorter period set later applies from the last reminder. Reminders are queued by the Offline sweep pass itself (every Report interval), not a separate timer. A reminder already queued but not yet sent is deleted, unsent, if the incident is acknowledged or ends first, so an acknowledgement inside the coalescing window still stops it. In one batch, any other news about the same incident (opened, worse, closed) takes the reminder's place. The subject follows the ticket's wording and carries no peak figure; the email names how to stop reminders. The reminder period lives in the email group in the deploy scripts (`NOTIFY_KEYS`), so it is set by its flag, not `--set`. Settings, Notifications does not show the period (only `deploy.sh info` does).

**Tested.** `notifier.test.ts` gains "reminders for long incidents" (8) at the database seam, through the real sweep and sender and a test relay: one reminder per period counted from the start (none a second before, one at 4 h, none again in the same period, the next at 8 h from the start, not 4 h after the first), emailed as `Still open: CHS IDF 2 Hot critical, 4 h`; a gap of three periods queues one, stored on the schedule (12 h), subject `13 h`; none after acknowledgement, and one queued before it is dropped unsent; none after close, and one queued before the close gives way to the closing email; a restart neither repeats one nor restarts the count (a 3 h old incident is reminded 4 h after its own start); the Bench gets none; off by default; a reminder and an Offline opening coalesce into `2 incidents: 1 Hot, 1 Offline (1 still open)`. The pure `reminderDue` (on schedule, late pass, missed periods, a shortened period, off). `notificationEmail.test.ts` (4 more): the lone subject and lines, whole hours and days, the digest counts, a reminder giving way to worse or closed, `openFor`. `config.test.ts`: the default 0, 4 read, empty and 0 off, 168 accepted, ignored without `SMTP_HOST`, refused at -1, 1.5 and 169. Migrations tests list 0013. Deploy tests (bash and PowerShell): Compose passes it and `.env.example` lists it, empty after a plain install; set to 4 with the other flags and shown by `info`; refused at 169, 1.5 and -4 naming the flag, leaving `.env` untouched; `--notify-remind-hours 0` alone changes only it and `info` says `reminders off`; `--smtp-host off` empties it and refuses it alongside; refused with email off.
