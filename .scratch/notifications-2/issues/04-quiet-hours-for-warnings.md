# 04 — Quiet hours for warnings

**What to build:**
Warning-level emails held during quiet hours (nights and weekends) go out as one digest when the quiet hours end. Critical incidents, Offline, and Sensor fault always send at once. A warning that rises to critical inside quiet hours sends at once, as `worse`.

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** done

**GitHub:** #14

- [x] `NOTIFY_QUIET_HOURS` (e.g. `18:00-07:00`) and `NOTIFY_QUIET_WEEKENDS` in `config.ts`, in the server's zone (`localDay.ts`), with deploy flags and `info`
- [x] The sender leaves warning rows due inside quiet hours pending, with a `not_before` time, and sends them in the first pass after; the 24 h give-up counts from `not_before`, not from queuing
- [x] An incident that opened and closed inside quiet hours arrives in the digest as "opened and resolved"
- [x] Settings status shows "N held until 07:00"
- [x] Tests drive the clock: held at 23:00, sent at 07:00, critical never held, a warning turned critical sends at once

**What was built.** `quietHours.ts` (pure): `parseDailyWindow` reads `18:00-07:00` (two different 24-hour times, the end before the start for a window past midnight) and `quietUntil(at, quiet, timeZone)` answers when the quiet ends if the instant is inside it, else null: the daily window, and with weekends all of Saturday and Sunday (Monday's midnight), windows that meet followed into each other, so Friday 18:00 waits until Monday 07:00. Wall-clock times come from `localDay.ts`, which now exports `wallClock`, adds `instantAfter` (the second reading of a time the clocks went back over, when the instant lies between them) and `weekdayOf`. `config.ts` reads `NOTIFY_QUIET_HOURS` and `NOTIFY_QUIET_WEEKENDS` into `notifications.quietHours` (`{daily, weekends}`; empty is none, refused otherwise with the other email problems, ignored without `SMTP_HOST`). `outbox.ts` gains the rule `heldByQuietHours`: a row waits when it is about an Incident other than Offline or Sensor fault and its level is not critical (openings, closes, and reminders alike, Mold risk high included); a held release and a monthly report never wait. `givesUp` takes the row's `not_before` and counts the day from the later of it and the queuing, and `readyToSend` treats a row quiet hours held as having waited its window. Migration `0019-quiet-hours` adds `notifications.not_before` (DATETIME(3), NULL), guarded. The sender (`notifier.ts`) claims due rows with their level and Condition (a LEFT JOIN on `incidents`, locking only the notifications: `FOR UPDATE OF n`); when the pass's own time is inside quiet hours, the warnings among them get `next_attempt_at` = the end and `not_before` = `COALESCE(not_before, end)` and leave the turn, so they are not claimed again until then and never fill a pass's 200. The first pass after sends them per recipient list as before, coalescing and per-list turns and retries unchanged. When an Incident row goes out, the rows quiet hours hold for the same Incident (`not_before` set, next try still ahead) are claimed alongside and marked sent with it. `outboxStatus(db, now)` adds `held` (rows whose `not_before` is still ahead, and the latest of them); `GET /api/notifications/status` adds `quietHours` (`{hours: '18:00-07:00' | null, weekends}`, null while off), `held`, and `heldUntil`. Settings, Notifications, shows "Quiet hours 18:00–07:00 and weekends: warnings wait until they end and go out together; critical Incidents, Offline, and Sensor fault are emailed at once." (or how to set them), "N notifications held until <time>, when quiet hours end.", and the waiting line counts the rest. `TZ` (coordinator decision, below): Compose passes `TZ: ${TZ:-UTC}` to api; `config.ts` refuses a `TZ` Node does not know. Deploy: `--notify-quiet-hours HH:MM-HH:MM|off` and `--notify-quiet-weekends on|off` in `deploy.sh` and `deploy.ps1` (in the email group, `NOTIFY_KEYS`: refused with email off and alongside `--smtp-host off`, emptied by it); the interactive email setup asks for both (offering off, and no); `info` and the install summary add `quiet hours 18:00-07:00 and weekends` (or `18:00-07:00`, `weekends`, `off`). `TZ` is a tunable (`--set TZ=America/Chicago`; shape checked, and on a host with `/usr/share/zoneinfo` that the host has the zone) and `info` prints `Time zone`, `UTC (TZ not set)` when empty. Both `.env.example` files list the three settings, commented. DEPLOYMENT.md, README (env paragraph and the status row), CONTEXT.md, ADR 0008, and the deploy skill updated.

**Decisions beyond the ticket.** Confirmed by the coordinator: Offline and Sensor fault always send at once, as the ticket says; the owner's "every warning waits, Humid included" means no warning Condition is exempt (Mold risk high counts as a warning), not that Offline waits. Decided by the coordinator, open to the owner: the containers run in UTC, so `api` now takes `TZ` from `.env`, which also moves the times written in emails and the monthly report's month bounds; empty leaves everything as it was. Mine: Compose passes `${TZ:-UTC}`, not `${TZ:-}`, since ICU takes an empty `TZ` for a zone it does not know rather than UTC. Rows are held by the sender at claim time, on the pass's clock, not at queueing, so a warning that became due at 17:59:30 and waited out the coalescing window past 18:00 waits for the morning, and so does a retry that falls inside quiet hours (no warning goes out in them); held rows are pushed out of the claim rather than re-checked every pass. A row held again after failing keeps its first `not_before`, so a relay down for days still gives a warning up a day after the quiet first let it go, rather than never. A warning that turns critical sends the `worse` email alone, so its subject says ", got worse" as the ticket asks, and the opening (or reminder) quiet hours held for that Incident is marked sent with it, unsent, so the morning does not repeat it; it is the only place rows are marked sent without being in the email. Released rows go in the first pass after the quiet, without waiting a coalescing window again. Settings counts as held only rows whose first `not_before` is still ahead; one held again after a failed try shows as waiting. Weekends are Saturday 00:00 to Monday 00:00; a daily window inside one day (`12:00-13:00`) works too; `00:00-00:00` and equal times are refused. Turning quiet hours off does not release rows already held: they go at the end they were given. The emails do not say they were held. The deploy scripts cannot know what Node knows, so `api` refusing an unknown `TZ` at startup is the final check.
