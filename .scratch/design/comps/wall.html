About this comp

Audience
An unattended NOC or office wall screen, read by whoever walks past: technicians, the help desk, a supervisor across the room.
Job
Answer one question from ten metres away: is every closet calm? If not, which one, how bad, and since when.
Key decision
A status band is the screen's sentence. Calm, it says "All calm" in grey. A new Condition replaces that sentence once, with a single wipe, and then holds still: the age ("3 min ago") is what tells the room it is new, not a blink. Campuses are columns, worst first, so the trouble is always top left.
Relies on
  • Quiet until it matters: calm is near monochrome.
  • Worst Wins: the column takes the critical border; only the closet row takes the solid fill.
  • Two Reds: Critical Fill under white, Critical Red only as a border.
  • No blinking indicators; the pulsing border stays gone.
  • Display sizing, extended: readouts scale with the screen (container units), not fixed at 48 to 60px.
Would change
  • DESIGN.md needs a "wall screen" exception to the 48 to 60px Display range and to the sidebar shell (a kiosk route has none).
  • The Critical Fill on a closet row is a new use: today it only fills a badge and a destructive button.
  • PRODUCT.md calls the wall screen a secondary case; building this makes it a designed one.
Server needs
Nothing new: Conditions, levels, thresholds and worst-first order already come from /api/dashboard and the stream. "Started" needs the Condition's start time, which the incident log (comp 3) would provide.
